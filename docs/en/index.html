How it plans

From seed to flower, in seven steps.

It doesn’t start with a feature list. It starts with “who uses this, on what kind of day?”

1

The core

3–6 things people will do with it

2

Imagine users

Five made-up people

3

Sketch a day

Their day, scene by scene

4

Find the needs

Features each scene needs

5

Score them

Higher when more people need it

6

Set the order

Highest score first

7

Split into stages

Stage 1, stage 2… the roadmap

Scores aren’t eyeballed by the AI. A script counts them, so you get the same answer every time.

Things to say

No commands to memorize.

“Plan this from scratch”Decides with you what to build, then writes the roadmap.
“Add this kind of user: an office worker who only plays on weekends”Adds them to the plan and only updates what changes.
“Build it by the roadmap”Hands the remaining work to teammates and asks before adding it.
“I’m going to bed”Asks once before you leave, then keeps working. Your decisions wait for the morning.
“How far along is the roadmap?”Briefly tells you what’s done and what’s next.
